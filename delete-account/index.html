Deleting your Split Bills account

Deleting your account removes it, and every record stored on the server, immediately and for good. The records on your phone are not affected: the app keeps working without an account.

In the app

  1. Open Settings, then tap your name under Account.
  2. Tap Delete account at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Confirm with Delete.

Any other phone signed in to the account is signed out.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email zkwokleung@gmail.com from the Google address you signed in with, with the subject “Delete my account”. We will delete the account and confirm by reply. Nothing else is needed; the sender address is how we know the account is yours.

What is deleted

Your Google identity as we hold it (email, name, profile photo link, account id) and every record that was synced: people, groups, categories, expenses, repayments and reminder templates. The server keeps no backups, so there is nothing left to purge later.

What is not

Anything on your phone. If you also want that gone, uninstall the app, or clear its data in your phone's settings.
